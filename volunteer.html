How to Check Your Bank Balance

  1. Open your official banking app (e.g. SBI YONO, HDFC Mobile Banking, Canara ai1).
  2. Log in using your registered mobile number and MPIN.
  3. Tap "Account Summary" or "Balance Enquiry".
  4. Your current balance will be shown on screen.
  5. Without internet: Dial *99# on any mobile phone. Follow the menu to check your balance using USSD.
ⓘ Aasara does not connect to any bank account. The guide above uses official bank apps only.
